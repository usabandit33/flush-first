Common questions

Is a tankless error code always a new unit?

No. Scale, a dirty flame rod, or a missed annual flush often throws the same codes shops use to quote replacement. Ask for a flush and diagnosis first.

How often do Houston tankless units need a flush?

Shops that publish maintenance pages typically talk about an annual or 12-to-18-month descale in this water. Ask what they will do on this visit.

Is this the same checklist as who pulls the water-heater permit?

No. Permit Pulled is Denver tank-unit permit work. This page is Houston tankless: flush and descale versus replace.

Why do tankless install quotes jump past three thousand dollars?

A new condensing unit plus gas-line and vent changes is a different job than a service flush. Ask which of those lines is on the quote.
